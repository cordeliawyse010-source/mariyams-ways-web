OUR WORK

Programmes That Support People and Communities

Through education, skills development, empowerment, outreach and welfare support, Mariyam’s Ways Foundation works to create opportunities and respond to community needs.

WHAT WE DO

Our Programmes & Activities

Our programmes are designed to support vulnerable people, build skills and create opportunities for individuals and communities.

EDUCATION

Educational Support

Providing educational materials, mentorship and other forms of support to students from disadvantaged backgrounds.

SKILLS

Skills Training

Practical training in areas such as tailoring, catering, hairdressing, digital skills and entrepreneurship.

EMPOWERMENT

Women & Youth Empowerment

Helping women and young people build knowledge, skills, confidence and greater economic independence.

OUTREACH

Community Outreach

Reaching communities directly to identify needs and provide appropriate support to individuals and families.

WELFARE

Donation & Welfare Programmes

Providing food, clothing, educational materials and other essential items to vulnerable people and families.

AWARENESS

Awareness Campaigns

Raising awareness around education, sanitation, women’s empowerment, child protection and community wellbeing.

WHO WE SUPPORT

Supporting People Who Need Opportunity Most

Our programmes focus on people and communities facing limited access to education, resources and economic opportunities.

Underserved Communities

Reaching communities with limited access to resources.

Children & Students

Especially those from disadvantaged backgrounds.

Young People

Supporting education, skills and personal development.

Vulnerable Families

Providing welfare and community-based support.

Women

Creating opportunities for empowerment and independence.

People Facing Economic Hardship

Helping individuals access support and opportunities.
